# PassPTCE Exam Blueprint

Encoded from PTCB PTCE Outline v1.4 (effective January 6, 2026).

PassPTCE is not affiliated with PTCB. All practice items are original.

## Domains and diagnostic weights

| Domain | Diagnostic questions | Weight |
|--------|---------------------:|-------:|
| Medications | 35 | 35% |
| Federal Requirements | 19 | 19% |
| Patient Safety and Quality Assurance | 24 | 24% |
| Order Entry and Processing | 22 | 22% |
| **Total** | **100** | **100%** |

## Coverage map (PassPTCE content)

### Medications (35 diagnostic)
- Brand/generic pairs (top dispensed and high-alert)
- Therapeutic classes and common indications
- Dosage forms and routes
- Controlled substance schedules I–V recognition
- REMS awareness (purpose, examples at high level)
- Side effects / counseling points at technician level

### Federal Requirements (19 diagnostic)
- Controlled Substances Act basics and schedules
- Pseudoephedrine purchase limits (Combat Methamphetamine Epidemic Act)
- DSCSA: product tracing, suspect/illegitimate product concepts
- HIPAA privacy and minimum necessary
- FDA recall classes (I, II, III) at awareness level
- DEA forms awareness (e.g., 222 concept) without procedural how-to for diversion

### Patient Safety and Quality Assurance (24 diagnostic)
- LASA (look-alike/sound-alike) pairs
- High-alert medications
- Error prevention strategies (tall man lettering, barcode, independent double-check concepts)
- Quality assurance / continuous improvement awareness
- Adverse event and near-miss reporting concepts
- Infection control and sterile vs nonsterile awareness (technician scope)

### Order Entry and Processing (22 diagnostic)
- Sig code interpretation
- Days supply calculations
- Dosage calculations (verify arithmetic in bank tests)
- NDC structure concepts (labeler / product / package)
- Refills, transfers awareness at federal level
- Insurance / adjudication basics (reject codes awareness, not payer-specific hacks)

## Diagnostic design
- Exactly 100 questions matching the table above
- Optional timer: 105 minutes
- Results: overall + per-domain weak / ok / strong
- Study plan prioritizes domain weight × weakness

## Paid content
- Additional original items (≥100) gated behind unlock
