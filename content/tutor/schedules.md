# Controlled Substance Schedules — PassPTCE Original Table

Federal scheduling awareness for PTCE study. States may differ. Not legal advice.

| Schedule | Abuse potential (relative) | Medical use (federal concept) | Tech recognition examples | Refill theme (federal teaching) |
|----------|----------------------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|
| I | Highest | No accepted medical use federally | Heroin, LSD, marijuana federally (policy evolving; exam may still test classic framing) | Not dispensed as usual Rx |
| II | High | Accepted, with restrictions | Oxycodone, hydrocodone combo products, morphine, fentanyl, methylphenidate, amphetamine salts, methadone | No federal refills |
| III | Less than II | Accepted | Products with limited opioids historically; anabolic steroids; ketamine | Limited refills in a time window |
| IV | Less than III | Accepted | Alprazolam, lorazepam, diazepam, clonazepam, zolpidem, tramadol, carisoprodol | Limited refills in a time window |
| V | Lowest among schedules | Accepted | Pregabalin; some codeine cough syrups within limits | May have more flexible rules; still controlled |

## Storage & handling themes
- C-II often secured with stricter access and perpetual inventory practices.
- Witness/documentation for destructions and significant losses.
- Know DEA 222 / CSOS = ordering C-I/II concept; Form 106 = theft/loss concept.

## Technician red flags (escalate, do not accuse alone)
- Early refill patterns without pharmacist review
- Altered written prescriptions
- Prescriber outside scope patterns
- Patient paying cash unusually for high-risk controls when insurance exists — context matters; pharmacist judgment

Stay inside tech role: document, notify pharmacist/PIC, follow SOP.
