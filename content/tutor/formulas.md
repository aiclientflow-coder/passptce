# Formula Sheet — PassPTCE Original

Quick reference for Order Entry calculations and conversions. Original teaching sheet — not a PTCB publication.

## Mass / volume conversions
- 1 kg = 1000 g
- 1 g = 1000 mg
- 1 mg = 1000 mcg
- 1 L = 1000 mL
- 1 tsp = 5 mL
- 1 tbsp = 15 mL
- 1 fl oz ≈ 30 mL (pharmacy teaching approximation)
- 1 pint = 473 mL (often ~480 mL in exam rounding contexts — use stem values)
- 1 lb = 16 oz; patient weight lb → kg: divide by 2.2
- 1 inch = 2.54 cm

## Days supply
- Tablets/capsules: quantity ÷ doses_per_day
- Liquid: total_mL ÷ mL_per_day
- Insulin: total_units_in_container ÷ units_per_day
- Inhaler: total_actuations ÷ actuations_per_day

## Dose calculations
- Tablets per dose = ordered_mg ÷ mg_per_tablet
- mL per dose = ordered_mg ÷ (mg_per_mL)
- mg dose = weight_kg × mg_per_kg
- Infusion (tech awareness): mL/hr = total_mL ÷ hours (pump programming is site/policy specific)

## Concentration bridges
- Percent w/v: X% = X g / 100 mL
- 1% = 10 mg/mL
- Ratio 1:N → 1 g / N mL
- Dilution (alligation / C1V1=C2V2): C1×V1 = C2×V2 when mixing same solute

## Temperature (storage awareness)
- Freezer / refrigerator / controlled room temperature ranges are USP-defined; exam may ask which products typically refrigerate (e.g., some insulins after opening rules vary by product — follow label).
- °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9; °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

## PSE limit numbers (federal retail)
- 3.6 g / day / purchaser
- 9 g / 30 days retail
- Mail-order commonly taught tighter monthly cap (7.5 g / 30 days)

## Roman numerals (label/Rx legacy)
- ss = 1/2; I=1; V=5; X=10; L=50; C=100
